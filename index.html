Midlight TV Uploader

The private publishing tool behind the Midlight TV channel on YouTube.

▶ YouTube This app uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to one YouTube channel.

Midlight TV (youtube.com/@midlight-tv) is a twice-weekly AI news show on YouTube, followed by three portfolio projects you can build for $0. This tool is used only by the channel owner to upload episodes to the owner's own YouTube channel for review, with their title, description, thumbnail and captions, and to add them to the channel's episode playlist.

It has no public sign-up, is not offered to other users and does not access anyone else's data.
