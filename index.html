Midlight TV Uploader

The private publishing tool behind the Midlight TV YouTube channel.

Midlight TV is a twice-weekly AI news show followed by three portfolio projects you can build for $0. This tool is used only by the channel owner to upload episodes to the owner's own YouTube channel (as unlisted drafts for human review) and to store the episode's files in the owner's own Google Drive.

It is not offered to other users and does not access anyone else's data.
