Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-09-27. Applies to the "Midlight TV Uploader" Google API client.

Who uses it

Only the owner of the Midlight TV channel. The app is not offered to the public.

What Google data it accesses

  • YouTube (scopes youtube.upload, youtube.force-ssl): uploads episodes to the owner's channel as unlisted or private drafts, sets thumbnails and captions, reads the status of its own uploads, manages the "All episodes" playlist, and deletes superseded drafts it uploaded itself.
  • Google Drive (scope drive.file): creates and updates only the files and folders the app itself creates (episode renders and build-guide zips). It cannot see other Drive files.

How data is stored and shared

OAuth refresh tokens are stored as encrypted GitHub Actions secrets in the owner's repository. Episode text (scripts, metadata) is stored in that repository. No Google user data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Build-guide zips are shared publicly by link on purpose, because they are the free downloads promised in each video description.
